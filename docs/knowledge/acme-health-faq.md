# Acme Health Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

Acme Health is a fictional primary care clinic used for demos. Every name, address, phone
number, price and policy in this document is invented sample content.

Each answer below is written to stand on its own, so it still makes sense when retrieved
by itself.

---

## Hours and location

### What are Acme Health's opening hours?

Acme Health is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, and on Saturdays from 9:00 am
to 1:00 pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on major public holidays. Phone lines open at
the same times as the clinic.

### Is Acme Health open on holidays?

Acme Health is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On the day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve, the
clinic closes early at 2:00 pm. Holiday closures are also posted at the front entrance.

### Where is Acme Health located?

Acme Health is at 100 Main Street, Suite 200. The entrance to the building is on the Elm Street
side, not on Main Street. Suite 200 is on the second floor; take the elevator directly ahead of
the Elm Street entrance.

### Where do patients park at Acme Health?

Free patient parking is in the garage on 2nd Street, one block from the clinic. Bring your
parking ticket to the front desk and staff will validate it, so parking is free for the length
of your visit. Accessible parking spaces are on the garage's ground level, closest to the exit.

### Can I get to Acme Health by public transit?

Yes. The Main and 3rd Street bus stop, served by routes 12 and 40, is a two-minute walk from the
clinic's Elm Street entrance. The nearest train station is Central Station, about ten minutes
away on foot.

### Is the Acme Health building wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The Elm Street entrance has a step-free ramp and automatic doors, the elevator reaches
Suite 200, and the clinic has an accessible restroom. If you need a wheelchair from the
entrance, ask the front desk when you book and one will be ready when you arrive.

## Appointments

### How do I book an appointment at Acme Health?

You can book an appointment by calling Acme Health and speaking with the automated assistant
or the front desk. Appointments are offered at 9:00 am, 10:30 am, 2:00 pm and 3:30 pm. To book,
change or cancel an appointment by phone, you'll be asked to confirm your full name and date of
birth first.

### How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment at Acme Health?

Call Acme Health to reschedule or cancel. You'll be asked to confirm your full name and date of
birth, and then you can move your appointment to another open time or cancel it. Please give at
least 24 hours' notice when you can.

### What is Acme Health's cancellation policy?

Acme Health asks for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancelling
with less than 24 hours' notice, or missing an appointment without calling, may result in a $25
late-cancellation fee. The fee is waived for emergencies and illness, and the first missed
appointment in a year is always waived.

### What happens if I'm late for my Acme Health appointment?

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, Acme Health may need to reschedule your appointment so
other patients aren't kept waiting. If you're running late, call the clinic and the front desk
will tell you whether you can still be seen.

### Does Acme Health offer same-day appointments?

Yes. Acme Health holds a small number of same-day appointments each weekday for urgent but
non-emergency problems, such as a fever, a minor infection or a sprain. Call as early as
possible after 8:00 am to ask for one. Same-day appointments are first come, first served.

### Does Acme Health offer telehealth or video visits?

Yes. Acme Health offers video visits for follow-ups, prescription questions and many minor
concerns. Video visits use the same appointment times as in-person visits. When you book, ask
for a video visit and you'll receive a link by text message before your appointment.

### What should I bring to my first Acme Health appointment?

For your first Acme Health visit, bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of the
medications you take (including vitamins and supplements), and any recent test results or
records from other doctors. Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete new-patient forms.

## Insurance and billing

### What insurance does Acme Health accept?

Acme Health accepts most major insurance plans, including most PPO and HMO plans and Medicare.
Bring your insurance card and a photo ID to every visit. Because plans change, call your
insurer to confirm Acme Health is in your plan's network before your first appointment.

### Can I be seen at Acme Health without insurance?

Yes. Acme Health sees patients without insurance at a self-pay rate. A standard office visit is
$120 for self-pay patients, and payment is due at the time of the visit. Ask the front desk
about a payment plan if you need one.

### How do I pay my Acme Health bill?

You can pay an Acme Health bill at the front desk by card, or by calling the billing office
during clinic hours. Acme Health accepts all major credit and debit cards and HSA and FSA cards.
Payment plans are available; ask the billing office.

### I think I was charged incorrectly. What should I do?

If you think an Acme Health bill is wrong, for example a duplicate charge or a charge your
insurer should have covered, ask to speak with the billing office. Have your bill and insurance
card ready. The billing office can review the charge, correct errors and resubmit claims to your
insurer.

### What is Acme Health's copay?

Your copay depends on your insurance plan, not on Acme Health. Your copay amount is usually
printed on your insurance card. Copays are collected at check-in.

## Prescriptions and records

### How do I request a prescription refill from Acme Health?

Ask your pharmacy to send a refill request to Acme Health, or call the clinic. Refill requests
are usually handled within two business days. Please request refills at least three days before
you run out. Some medications need a visit before they can be refilled.

### How do I get a copy of my medical records from Acme Health?

To get a copy of your Acme Health medical records, ask the front desk for a records request
form. You'll need to show a photo ID. Records are usually ready within five business days, and there is no charge
for records sent directly to another doctor.

### How do I get my test results from Acme Health?

Acme Health contacts you with test results, usually within three to five business days. If a
result needs follow-up, a clinician will call you. If you haven't heard back after a week, call
the clinic and ask the front desk to check on your results.

## Services

### What services does Acme Health offer?

Acme Health provides primary care for adults and children: annual physicals, sick visits,
vaccinations, chronic condition care (such as diabetes and high blood pressure), women's health,
minor procedures, and lab tests. For specialist care, Acme Health can refer you to a specialist.

### Does Acme Health offer flu shots and vaccines?

Yes. Acme Health offers flu shots every fall and winter, along with routine vaccines for adults
and children, including tetanus, shingles, pneumonia and childhood immunizations. Flu shots are
available during any appointment, and walk-in flu shots are offered on weekday afternoons from
2:00 pm to 5:00 pm during flu season.

### Does Acme Health have an on-site lab?

Yes. Acme Health has an on-site lab for blood draws and common tests. The lab is open weekdays
from 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. Some blood tests require fasting; your clinician will tell you if yours
does.

### Does Acme Health see children?

Yes. Acme Health sees patients of all ages, including newborns, children and teenagers, for
check-ups, vaccinations and sick visits. A parent or guardian must come with patients under 18.

## Urgent and emergency care

### What should I do in a medical emergency?

If you or someone else is having a medical emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing,
severe bleeding, signs of a stroke, or a serious injury, hang up and call 911 right away. Do not
wait for a call back from the clinic. Acme Health is not an emergency room.

### What should I do if I need care after Acme Health is closed?

If you need care when Acme Health is closed and it is not an emergency, you can visit an urgent
care center. For any emergency, call 911. When the clinic opens, call to let your Acme Health
care team know about your visit so they can follow up.

### Can the Acme Health phone assistant give medical advice?

No. The Acme Health automated phone assistant can answer general questions about the clinic and
help you book, change or cancel appointments, but it cannot give medical advice, a diagnosis, or
tell you how urgent a symptom is. For medical questions, ask to speak with a person at the
office. For an emergency, call 911.

## Contacting Acme Health

### How do I talk to a person at Acme Health?

When you call Acme Health, you can ask the automated assistant to connect you with a person at
any time and it will transfer your call to the front desk. If no one is available, you'll be
offered a call back or the option to leave a message.

### Can I leave a message for my Acme Health doctor?

Yes. Call Acme Health and ask to leave a message for your care team. Messages are reviewed during
clinic hours, and non-urgent messages are usually answered within one business day. Do not leave
a message for anything urgent; for an emergency, call 911.

### What languages does Acme Health support?

Acme Health's front desk and clinicians speak English and Spanish. Free interpreter services are
available for other languages, including by phone during your appointment. Ask for an
interpreter when you book.
